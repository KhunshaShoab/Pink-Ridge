Our story

Something ancient, handled with care.

Pink Ridge was built on a simple belief — that something as ancient as Himalayan salt deserves to be handled with care. Every lamp, block, and grain begins in the Himalayan salt range, mined and then hand-finished by skilled craftspeople who shape each piece individually.

We're not a mass warehouse. We're a team that takes pride in consistency — the right colour, the right finish, and packing that arrives intact whether it's going to a home down the road or a distributor across the ocean.
