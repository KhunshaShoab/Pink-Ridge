01 — The Origin

Beneath the
mountains.

For millions of years, Himalayan salt has remained sealed beneath the mountains of northern Pakistan — shaped by geological time and untouched by modern production.

Pink Ridge begins at that source, working with a naturally distinctive material and transforming it into products made for contemporary spaces and international markets.
