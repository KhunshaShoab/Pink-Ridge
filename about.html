The Origin

From the Heart of
the Himalayas.

Two hundred and fifty million years ago an ancient sea withdrew from what is now northern Pakistan, leaving behind a seam of crystalline salt that the rising Himalayas would seal beneath stone.

That seam is where our work begins. The salt is cut in blocks from deep galleries, brought to the surface unwashed and unbleached, and sorted by hand before it ever reaches a workshop.

Nothing is added. Nothing is dyed. What arrives at your door is what the mountain made.

TIME. MINERAL. EARTH.
The Material

A Mineral Shaped
by Time.

Formed over millions of years under immense pressure, Himalayan salt carries a mineral signature no laboratory reproduces.

The Makers

Crafted by Hands.
Defined by Care.

Every piece passes through the hands of people who have shaped this stone for years — often for a generation before that.

Machines can cut salt. They cannot read it. Choosing where a block will split, how a surface should be finished, which pieces meet the standard and which do not — that judgement is human, and it is the reason our work is consistent.

Consistency is not a promise we make once. It is the same decision, made carefully, thousands of times.
