Salt Bricks & Tiles

For kitchens, spas, and design projects.

Cooking blocks, grilling slabs, and wall tiles for restaurants, spas, and design projects. Salt blocks bring a distinctive edge to a kitchen — used for searing, serving, and chilling — while wall tiles create warm, glowing salt features in spas and interiors.

Cut to consistent dimensions and finished for their purpose, with bevelled or squared edges as your project requires.

Packaging & export

Packed to arrive intact — anywhere.

Blocks and tiles are edge-protected and packed flat in reinforced cartons, then palletised — built to handle the weight and survive long-haul freight.

  • Individually wrapped, cushioned inner packing
  • Palletised master cartons for volume orders
  • Export documentation & labelling on request
  • Private-label and custom packaging available

Export availability: Worldwide, by sea or air freight. Flexible minimum order quantities.
